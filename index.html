Our Story

Heritage as Investment

Jun Yan Investment & Art bridges the world of classical Chinese cultural heritage and modern connoisseurship. We specialize in traditional calligraphy, stone seal rubbings (金石拓片), and ancient Buddhist artifacts — rare works of enduring historical and aesthetic significance.


Our collection spans Northern Wei Buddhist stone carvings, Han dynasty painted tomb tiles, and contemporary masters of the classical ink arts. Each work is selected for its authenticity, provenance, and the depth of civilization it carries.

Collection

Calligraphy & Seal Art
金石书法

Stone seal rubbings are among China's oldest art forms — capturing inscriptions from ancient bronze vessels, stone stelae, and carved seals. Combined with classical calligraphy, these works document millennia of Chinese civilization, spiritual practice, and philosophical thought.

Collection

Antiquities & Artifacts
珍贵文物

Our antiquities collection encompasses Buddhist stone sculptures from the Northern Wei, Tang, and Song dynasties, alongside Han dynasty painted tomb tiles and rare jade pieces. Each artifact is a fragment of history preserved across centuries — objects of scholarly significance and timeless beauty.
